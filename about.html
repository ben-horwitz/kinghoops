KING Hoops is a sports-based youth development organization serving student-athletes across New York City. We develop great people first and great players second, using basketball to build confidence, character, and a path to college.

Representing all five boroughs, our athletes find more than a team. They find community, mentorship, and opportunities that last well beyond the final buzzer.

Who we are

Our Story

KING Hoops is a sports-based youth development organization serving student-athletes across New York City. We believe that every young person deserves access to opportunity, positive mentors, and a community that believes in their potential. Through basketball, we help young people develop the confidence, character, and life skills needed to succeed both on and off the court.

With the goal of helping every player pursue higher education, KING Hoops focuses on the holistic development of each student-athlete - developing great people first and great basketball players second. In addition to practices, skill development, leagues, and travel tournaments, athletes participate in academic support, life skills workshops, college and career readiness programming, mentorship, and community engagement opportunities. Every aspect of our program is intentionally designed to prepare our players for success in college, their careers, and their communities.

With participants representing all five boroughs of New York City and a wide range of racial, cultural, and socioeconomic backgrounds, KING Hoops uses basketball to bring communities together. We believe basketball has the power to build lasting relationships, foster belonging, and create opportunities that extend well beyond the final buzzer.

Since 2016

Our History

2016

KING Hoops was founded as an AAU program under the community-based organization Kids in the Game (at the time branded as KING, hence the name KING Hoops!). With baby-blue jerseys and Coach Michael, Floyd and Paul at the helm, we began to build the KING Hoops of today.

2019

KING Hoops continues to grow on and off the court with sending our first full graduating class to college and officially launching our formal academic program through support of the Heisman Trophy Fund.

2021

Showed strength and resilience during the pandemic to come out stronger and joining the Under Armour Future circuit.

2023

With the support of Kids in the Game, KING Hoops branched away from the larger branded and launched as an independent 501c3 non-profit entity.

Current

Today, KING Hoops serves more than 150 student-athletes annually, provides over 200 hours of academic and life skills support, maintains a 100% high school graduation rate, and continues to develop great people first and great basketball players second.

Growth

Since its founding, KING Hoops has grown from a grassroots basketball program into a thriving sports-based youth development organization. What began as a vision to create opportunities through sport has evolved into a community that provides mentorship, academic support, life skills programming, and pathways to higher education.

As the organization has expanded, so has its impact. Through strong partnerships, dedicated volunteers, and a commitment to meeting the needs of young people, KING Hoops continues to grow while remaining focused on its mission of helping every student-athlete reach their full potential, both on and off the court.

The road ahead

Strategic Plan

As KING Hoops continues to grow, our Strategic Plan serves as a roadmap for the future. Built around three strategic priorities - Enhance, Scale, and Retain - the plan reflects our commitment to strengthening our programs, expanding our reach, and creating lasting opportunities.

Enhance

Elevate the quality of every athlete’s experience by investing in coaching excellence, academic support, mentorship, and program resources.

Scale

Expand our impact through strategic partnerships, sustainable funding, and thoughtful organizational growth that allows us to reach more young people.

Retain

Strengthen lifelong connections with our athletes, families, alumni, and supporters to build a community that continues to inspire and give back.
