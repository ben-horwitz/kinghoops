Who we are

KING Hoops is a sports-based youth development organization serving student-athletes across New York City. We believe that every young person deserves access to opportunity, positive mentors, and a community that believes in their potential.

Our Story
Program 01

AAU Programs

KING Hoops offers competitive basketball teams for 6th-12th graders, competing locally, regionally and nationally. Our teams are coached by high quality, dedicated coaches and are provided with skill development on and off the court.

Learn More →
Program 02

Fall Academy

KING Hoops’ Fall Academy is a targeted program for high school student-athletes with aspirations to play college basketball. The program features strength and conditioning, targeted workshops, and college exploration.

Program 03

Clinics

KING Hoops offers a variety of local clinics introducing youth to the fundamentals of basketball in a fun, approachable environment.

Since 2016

KING Hoops was founded as an AAU program under the community-based organization Kids in the Game (at the time branded as KING, hence the name KING Hoops!).

Today, KING Hoops serves more than 150 student-athletes annually, provides over 200 hours of academic and life skills support, and maintains a 100% high school graduation rate.

Our Events

The Shoot-A-Thon

Each Fall, KING Hoops hosts our annual Shoot-A-Thon, bringing our community together around one mission: helping our student-athletes succeed on and off the court. Every dollar raised directly supports academic programming, college readiness, leadership development, and opportunities for our athletes.

View Event

March Madness Watch Party

Every March, KING Hoops brings our community together for our annual March Madness Watch Party. This signature event celebrates the excitement of the NCAA Basketball Tournament while raising critical scholarship funds that help our athletes pursue their dreams of competing at the next level.
