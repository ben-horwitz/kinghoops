Tryout Information

Tryout season is here! At KING Hoops, we hold tryouts throughout the year to give athletes multiple opportunities to join our program:

Fall12U–15U teams
WinterMiddle school programming
SpringFull program (12U–17U)

Our tryout process consists of two evaluation sessions, each lasting approximately 90 minutes to 2 hours. During those sessions, our coaching staff evaluates athletes not only on their basketball ability, but also on their coachability, effort, attitude, competitiveness, and how they fit within the culture of our program.

Our goal isn't simply to select the 10 or 11 most talented players. We're building teams.

That means finding athletes whose skills complement one another and who bring value both on and off the court. The best teams are made up of players who embrace different roles, compete selflessly, and contribute to a positive team culture.
