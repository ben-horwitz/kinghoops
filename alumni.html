Alumni

Over the last 3 seasons, KING Hoops has sent over 20+ student-athletes to play college basketball. Since its inception, KING Hoops has sent 65+ college athletes. Most importantly, we have a 100% graduation rate, and 92% of our participants go to college! Here are some of our most recent alumni from the last few years!
