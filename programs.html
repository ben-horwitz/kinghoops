AAU Program

At our core, KING Hoops is a competitive basketball program consisting of three components:

Basketball TeamsAcademic SupportCareer Development Cohort
01

Basketball

High-level coaching and competition designed to maximize each athlete's development while building confidence, leadership, and character.

  • Competitive AAU teams (Grades 6–12)
  • Elite local, regional & national competition
  • Skill development & basketball IQ
  • Experienced coaches & mentorship
  • College exposure opportunities
20+

Quick Stat: student-athletes from the last three graduating classes have gone on to play college basketball.

02

Academic

Supporting student-athletes beyond basketball through academic enrichment, mentorship, and life-skills programming that prepares them for success in school and life.

  • Weekly academic & life-skills sessions
  • Study halls & academic support
  • Leadership & character development
  • College preparation & recruiting guidance
  • Financial literacy & social-emotional learning
03

Career Development

Providing high school student-athletes with meaningful work experiences, professional mentorship, and career exploration opportunities.

  • Paid internships & employment opportunities
  • Resume & interview preparation
  • Career exploration & networking
  • Leadership & professional development
  • Hands-on experience with community partners

Beyond the season

Additional Programs

Fall Academy

An 8-week intensive program designed to help high school athletes prepare for their upcoming season through elite performance training, basketball development, college exposure, and life skills.

Athletes Receive:

Basketball Performance Training
  • Strength & Conditioning
  • Plyometrics
  • VertiMax Training
High-Level Skill Development
  • Basketball IQ & Decision-Making
  • Shooting & Finishing
  • Small-Sided Play & Game Situations
College & Life Preparation
  • Workshops covering college recruiting, nutrition, mindfulness, financial literacy, leadership, and social media
  • College campus visits and exposure to collegiate athletics
  • Opportunities to learn from professionals working throughout the sports industry

Free Community Clinics

Free and low-cost basketball clinics that make the game accessible to young athletes across New York City. Open to boys and girls of all skill levels.

Participants Receive:

Fundamental Skill Development
  • Basketball fundamentals in a fun, positive environment
Community Programming
  • Free summer clinics at Brooklyn Bridge Park
  • Weekly programs with SNL, Kids in the Game, and community partners
Positive Mentorship
  • Led by experienced KING Hoops coaches and athletes
  • Focus on confidence, teamwork, and character

Our Community Clinics help young athletes develop on and off the court while fostering a lifelong love for the game.

Competition With Purpose

Tournament Schedule

At KING Hoops, every tournament is selected with a purpose. Our goal is to provide the right balance of competition, development, and exposure while creating an environment where athletes can continue to improve throughout the season.

Our Philosophy

We believe more basketball does not always mean better development. Rather than filling every weekend with games, we intentionally build our schedule around:

  • Competitive, meaningful games
  • Consistent practices and team development
  • Individual skill development
  • Strength and conditioning
  • Recovery and long-term player growth

We believe athletes develop the most when high-level competition is paired with consistent practice/training. Our goal is to compete in some of the best events in the Northeast while giving our players the time and resources they need to improve on and off the court.

High School (15U–17U)

  • Compete in premier local, regional, and national events.
  • Gain exposure to college coaches from all levels.
  • Events include MADE Hoops, Hoop Group, Zero Gravity, Gym Rat Challenge, A Game, and other top tournaments throughout the Northeast.

Middle School (12U–14U)

  • Compete in highly competitive local and regional events.
  • Many teams participate in the UA Future Circuit, facing top programs from across the Northeast in our Spring season.
  • Additional events include MADE Hoops, Zero Gravity, and other premier local tournaments.
